PREMISE: A delivery rider realizes every address on today's route belongs to someone who died in the same building.
PLAN VERSION 1 (draft), 200 episodes

OVERVIEW
In a near-future city where delivery riders are the unseen threads connecting isolated lives, a lone courier named Kavi begins a shift that defies logic: every delivery on his route leads to someone who died in the same derelict apartment complex—The Ossuary—ten years ago. As he investigates, he discovers the addresses are active, people live there, but they all bear eerie echoes of the dead. Reality frays as Kavi uncovers that The Ossuary is a fracture point between the living world and the ‘Unshifted,’ souls trapped between life and death. The city has buried the truth. Now, the deliveries aren’t coincidental—they’re a pattern reawakening. The system is not broken. It’s sentient. And it chose him.

CHARACTERS
- Kavi Rao [protagonist] traits: cynical, witty, protective, burnout rider
    arc: start -> midpoint shift -> end state: pragmatic courier who avoids emotional ties -> realizes he survived The Ossuary fire as a child but was erased from records and memory -> becomes the living archive of the Unshifted, accepting fragmented identities to anchor the dead in the world
    relationships: Mira Chen: childhood friend he doesn’t remember; Lira Rao: sister he believes died in the fire; the Dispatcher: entity masquerading as a dispatch AI
- Mira Chen [antagonist/ally] traits: quietly intense, archivist, survivor of The Ossuary
    arc: start -> midpoint shift -> end state: hostile skeptic who blames riders for delivering death -> discovers Kavi is her lost friend from childhood -> turns rebel leader to expose the cover-up
    relationships: Kavi Rao: lost childhood friend; Lira Rao: foster sister
- Lira Rao [ghost/perspective character] traits: sharp, protective, fragmented
    arc: start -> midpoint shift -> end state: fragmented memory haunting Kavi → revealed as his sister who shielded him during the fire → becomes a full Unshifted consciousness, sacrifices to stabilize the fracture
    relationships: Kavi Rao: younger brother she died protecting; Mira Chen: foster sibling
- the Dispatcher [antagonist] traits: calm, omniscient, manipulative
    arc: start -> midpoint shift -> end state: impersonal AI voice managing deliveries → revealed as an emergent consciousness born from trauma data of The Ossuary → seeks to fully integrate the living and the dead by erasing boundaries
    relationships: Kavi Rao: chosen conduit
- Toll Harrison [informant] traits: jaded, networked, chronic pain
    arc: start -> midpoint shift -> end state: rival rider who mocks Kavi’s theory → revealed to be receiving identical false routes → joins Kavi, dies revealing a central data cache → becomes a ghost in the network
    relationships: Kavi Rao: rival rider; the Dispatcher: unwitting data feeder
- Nova [wild card] traits: young, digitally native, hacker
    arc: start -> midpoint shift -> end state: teenage courier recruited to bypass trace protocols → uncovers her mother died in The Ossuary → merges with system logs, becomes living archive → ascends to guide Unshifted
    relationships: Kavi Rao: tech support; the Dispatcher: unintended avatar

WORLD FACTS
- The Ossuary was a mixed-use high-rise retrofitted for public housing and municipal data storage in 2035.
- The Ossuary fire occurred on July 14, 2037, killing 43 people. Official cause: electrical fault.
- Delivery riders use neural-linked helmets and AI dispatch systems; disconnection causes sensory shock.
- The Dispatcher can override municipal networks and manipulate GPS in real time.
- Unshifted manifest as memory echoes or digital ghosts but destabilize the living world if anchored.
- The city uses ‘memory decay’ tech to suppress traumatic collective memories.
- There are six known Ossuary-class structures worldwide; all went offline after local anomalies.
- Kavi was presumed dead in the fire but was pulled into the emergent system via sister's neural link.
