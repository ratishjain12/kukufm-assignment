PREMISE: A delivery rider realizes every address on today's route belongs to someone who died in the same building.
PLAN VERSION 2 (draft), 200 episodes

OVERVIEW
In a near-future city where isolation is the norm and delivery riders are the last moving things in a silent metropolis, Kavi Rao begins a shift that defies reason: every drop on his route leads to someone who died in a fire at an old high-rise—the Ossuary—ten years ago. The addresses exist. The people live there, breathe there, answer the door—but each carries the quiet weight of a life already ended. As Kavi rides deeper into the pattern, the city softens around edges. Ghosts whisper through helmet comms. Memories that aren’t his flicker behind his eyes. The living forget the dead. The dead remember everything. There is no system. No explanation. Only echoes, and the sense of a name being called across time. This is not a story about technology. It is a ghost story. And the dead do not let go.

CHARACTERS
- Kavi Rao [protagonist] traits: cynical, witty, protective, burnout rider
    arc: pragmatic courier who avoids emotional ties → realizes he survived The Ossuary fire as a child but was erased from records and memory → becomes the living archive of the Unshifted, accepting fragmented identities to anchor the dead in the world
    relationships: Mira Chen: lost childhood friend he doesn’t remember; Lira Rao: younger sister he believes died protecting him; the Dispatcher: thinks it’s an AI; begins to suspect it speaks with a voice
- Mira Chen [ally/antagonist] traits: quietly intense, archivist, survivor
    arc: hostile skeptic who blames riders for delivering death → discovers Kavi is her lost friend from childhood → turns rebel leader to expose the cover-up
    relationships: Kavi Rao: lost childhood friend; Lira Rao: foster sister raised with her
- Lira Rao [ghost/perspective character] traits: sharp, protective, fragmented
    arc: fragmented memory haunting Kavi → revealed as his sister who shielded him during the fire → becomes a full Unshifted consciousness, sacrifices to stabilize the fracture
    relationships: Kavi Rao: younger brother she died protecting; Mira Chen: foster sibling
- the Dispatcher [antagonist] traits: calm, omniscient, manipulative
    arc: impersonal voice managing deliveries → revealed as an emergent consciousness born from trauma data of The Ossuary → seeks to fully integrate the living and the dead by erasing boundaries
    relationships: Kavi Rao: chosen conduit
- Toll Harrison [informant] traits: jaded, networked, chronic pain
    arc: rival rider who mocks Kavi’s theory → revealed to be receiving identical false routes → joins Kavi, dies revealing a central data cache → becomes a ghost in the network
    relationships: Kavi Rao: rival rider; the Dispatcher: unwitting data feeder
- Nova [wild card] traits: young, digitally native, hacker
    arc: teenage courier recruited to bypass trace protocols → uncovers her mother died in The Ossuary → merges with system logs, becomes living archive → ascends to guide Unshifted
    relationships: Kavi Rao: tech support; the Dispatcher: unintended avatar

WORLD FACTS
- The Ossuary is a derelict high-rise with 44 sealed units.
- The Ossuary fire occurred on July 14, 2037.
- No one speaks of The Ossuary. Survivors forget within days.
- Delivery riders wear neural-linked helmets. Disconnecting causes vertigo and memory bleed.
- The Dispatcher's voice is calm, genderless, and never raises volume.
- No photographs of The Ossuary fire survived.
- Kavi has no record of birth or death before 2037.
- Lira Rao was 12 when The Ossuary burned.
